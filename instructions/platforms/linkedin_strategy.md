# LinkedIn Application Strategy

## Objective
Apply to high-quality software engineering roles on LinkedIn efficiently.

## Search Strategy
1. Navigate to LinkedIn Jobs.
2. Enter the target job titles defined in `/personal_data/profile.md` (e.g., "Frontend Engineer", "React Developer").
3. Filter by **Date Posted**: "Past 24 hours" (preferred) or "Past week".
4. Filter by **Easy Apply**.

## Execution Flow
1. Iterate through the search results.
2. Click on a job posting.
3. Click the **Easy Apply** button.
4. Auto-fill the required fields using data from `/personal_data/`.
5. If you encounter complex dropdowns or unexpected questions, try to map them to `/personal_data/form_answers.md`.
6. Submit the application.
7. Log the application to `/tracking/applied_jobs.csv`.
8. Move to the next job in the list.

## Exclusions
- Do not apply to roles that strictly require years of experience exceeding the user's profile.
- Skip applications that redirect to complex third-party external sites unless specified otherwise.
