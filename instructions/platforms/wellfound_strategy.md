# Wellfound (AngelList) Application Strategy

## Objective
Apply to startup roles on Wellfound by leveraging their unique pitching system.

## Search Strategy
1. Navigate to Wellfound jobs section.
2. Apply standard role and location filters.
3. Sort by Newest.

## Execution Flow
1. Select a job match.
2. Wellfound requires a short "Note" or "Pitch" to the founder/recruiter.
3. Generate a concise, enthusiastic 2-3 sentence pitch. 
   - Reference the user's core stack (from `/personal_data/profile.md`).
   - Keep it professional and direct (e.g., "Hi, I'm a Frontend Engineer with X years of experience building React apps. I'm excited about what you're building at [Company] and would love to contribute.").
4. Submit application.
5. Log to `/tracking/applied_jobs.csv`.

## Exclusions
- Do not write long, generic cover letters. Keep the pitch short and tailored to the fact that it's a startup.
